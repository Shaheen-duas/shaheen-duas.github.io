About Roz ki Dua

Roz ki Dua is a free and simple app made to help Muslims read, understand and remember duas in daily life. Every dua is shown with its Arabic text, an easy transliteration, an English meaning and a Hindi meaning, so that anyone can read it comfortably.

What you will find inside

The app has 78 duas. Most of them are duas that are mentioned in the Quran, and each one shows the name of the Surah and the Ayah number, and the name of the Prophet who made that dua where this is known. Some duas were taught by the Prophet Muhammad (peace be upon him) and are taken from well known Hadith books such as Sahih Bukhari, Sahih Muslim, Tirmidhi and Abu Dawood.

You can also open the Surah tab to read popular full Surahs such as Al-Fatiha, Al-Kahf, Yaseen, Al-Mulk and Al-Ikhlas, and listen to the recitation ayah by ayah.

How to use the app

Open the app and you will see five duas for the day. Read them slowly, think about their meaning and make dua with a sincere heart. You can use the search box to find a dua by its words, by the name of a Prophet or by the name of a Surah.

Why we made it

Many people want to learn duas but do not have one clean and easy place to read them. We built Roz ki Dua to be a light, easy to read app that works on any phone and does not ask you to sign up or log in.
